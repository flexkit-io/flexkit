Studio quality of life: the AI app is now the first tab and the default app of a project. Opened apps stay mounted while another app is in front, so switching from a chat to Desk, the Asset Manager or the Explorer and back finds the chat (and the other apps) exactly as they were left, with the app bar button returning to the page each app was last on. The agent composer also remembers unsent text per chat, including the New chat page, across chat switches and reloads.
