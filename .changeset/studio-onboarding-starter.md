Creating a project in the hosted and desktop Studio now asks how to start: the commerce starter (22 entities for catalog, inventory, orders, customers and marketing, with EU, UK and US markets, plus an optional Aven Living sample store), a description of the business that the AI agent turns into a schema, or an empty project. The new project's chat shows the starter's progress and then questions the sample store can answer. Email sign-ups are asked for their name first, and the email sign-in message reads naturally in the desktop app.
